              How CooWatch works

              Watching together

              Whoever starts a session is the host. They choose what plays: a file from their device, their screen or a browser tab, or a YouTube link. Everyone else receives it.

              With a file or a screen, the host's machine sends the picture directly to each viewer, so when they pause, it freezes for everybody. A YouTube session plays on each device separately and the clocks are kept together instead.

              Controls

              Play, pause and the seek bar work for everyone in the room, not just the host, unless the host locks them. Double-click the video or press F for fullscreen, Space to play or pause, and Enter to chat without leaving fullscreen.

              Talking

              Your microphone starts muted every time. Nothing is sent until you press the mic button. Type @ to tag someone, and click any message to reply to it.

              Wear headphones if you are the one sharing a file: otherwise its sound goes back out through your microphone as an echo.

              If you are the host

              The room panel lets you rename the session, close the chat, lock playback, mute everyone, and run a poll. Per person you can mute for 30 seconds or indefinitely, block them from the chat, hand over the host role, or remove them.

              Muting someone is a request their browser honours, not a hard block, so treat it as a courtesy rather than a guarantee. Someone who muted themselves stays muted; only they can undo that.

              Everyone else

              You can ask to become the host from the panel, and start a vote to remove someone if at least three people are watching. The host cannot be voted out.

              Second screen

              Joining from a phone as a second screen gives you chat and the playback controls without pulling the video again, so it costs the host no extra bandwidth. Handy for chatting while you watch on a laptop.

              Leaving

              Reloading keeps your place; the room holds your seat for a minute and a half. When the host leaves they choose whether to hand the room over or close it. Every session is saved to History on your own device, transcript included.
